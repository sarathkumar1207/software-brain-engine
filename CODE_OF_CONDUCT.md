# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Software Brain Engine is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people
- give actionable feedback and accept correction professionally
- respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, and deliberate disruption
- protect private information and follow responsible security disclosure

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels,
and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
<https://github.com/sarathkumar1207>. Do not use a public issue for sensitive conduct reports.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a
participant based on severity and repeated behavior. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community conduct practices and is maintained
as a project-specific enforcement policy.
